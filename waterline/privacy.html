Waterline: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Waterline ("the game") is published by Warairo Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information. Contact: warairo.studio@gmail.com

Short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or precise location.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, hints, settings) is stored on your device only.
  • Versions of the game that show ads use an advertising service that may collect device and advertising identifiers to show ads (see "Ads").

Information stored on your device

Your progress, stars, hint tokens, settings (language, theme, sound), daily reward state and the state of unfinished levels are saved in a file on your device. We do not receive this file. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Ads

Versions of Waterline that contain ads use Unity LevelPlay (by Unity Technologies / ironSource) to show interstitial and rewarded video ads (the rewarded ad gives you a hint). To do this, the ad service and its ad partners may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or switch off in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language) and information about how ads perform (views, taps, whether a rewarded ad was completed).

This information is used to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive your advertising ID. The advertising partners' own privacy policies apply to what they collect: Unity privacy policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the game's settings. Versions of the game that do not contain ads do not collect or share any advertising data.

Purchases

The game does not currently offer in-app purchases. If it does in future, purchases are handled by Google Play, and we only learn that a purchase was made, not your payment details.

Children

Waterline is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the advertising service described above, and only in versions that show ads.

Retention and your rights

We do not hold personal information about you ourselves. To stop ad data collection, reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, or decline personalised ads when asked. If you are in the EEA, UK or another region with data protection rights, you can contact us at the address above and we will help with any request, including requests directed at our advertising partners.

Security

Progress is stored in the game's private storage on your device. Ad data is handled by the advertising service under its own security practices.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If the game starts collecting new kinds of information, we will say so here and in the game before it does.
